## Support & Training

Successful SAP adoption doesn't end at go-live — it continues through ongoing support and continuous learning. ALFA Business Technologies provides comprehensive post-implementation support and training services to ensure your SAP investment delivers lasting value.

## Application Management Services (AMS)

Our AMS offering provides structured, SLA-based support for your SAP environment:

### Helpdesk Support
- Tiered support model (L1, L2, L3)
- Incident management and tracking
- Response time SLAs based on business criticality
- 24/7 monitoring options for critical systems

### Functional Support
- Business process issue resolution
- Configuration adjustments for changed business requirements
- Period-end closing support (month-end, year-end)
- Regulatory update implementation

### Technical Support
- SAP Basis administration and monitoring
- System performance management
- Security and authorization support
- Transport management

### Continuous Improvement
- Quarterly system health checks
- SAP note and patch application
- Minor enhancement requests
- Business process optimization recommendations

## Training Services

ALFA consultants deliver training in the areas where they have deep expertise:

### SAP Standard Course Training
- Delivery of SAP's official training catalog courses
- Authorized training center network partnership
- Courses available for all major SAP modules

### Custom Workshop Training
- Tailored for your specific SAP configuration and processes
- Delivered at your site or remotely
- Role-based training (end users, key users, IT staff)
- Training documentation customized for your system

### Training Formats
- Classroom training (on-site or at ALFA premises)
- Virtual / online instructor-led training
- eLearning module development
- Train-the-trainer programs
