## SAP HANA & S/4HANA

SAP S/4HANA represents the future of enterprise resource planning — a next-generation ERP suite built on SAP's in-memory HANA database platform. ALFA Business Technologies guides enterprises through every phase of their S/4HANA journey.

## What is SAP S/4HANA?

SAP S/4HANA is SAP's intelligent, next-generation ERP system that delivers:
- Real-time analytics and reporting alongside operational processing
- Simplified data model for faster performance
- Modern Fiori user experience
- Embedded AI and machine learning capabilities
- Cloud, on-premise, and hybrid deployment options

## Our S/4HANA Services

### Migration Paths

**Greenfield (New Implementation)**
- Start fresh with SAP S/4HANA best practices
- Clean slate approach with optimized processes
- Ideal for organizations wanting to redesign processes

**System Conversion (Brownfield)**
- Migrate existing SAP ERP to S/4HANA
- Preserve existing data, customizations, and history
- Structured conversion with minimal business disruption

**Selective Data Transition**
- Combine elements of both approaches
- Migrate selective data while adopting new processes
