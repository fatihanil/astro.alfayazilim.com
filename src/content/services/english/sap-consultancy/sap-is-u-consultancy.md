## SAP IS-U Consultancy

SAP IS-U (Industry Solution Utilities) is a specialized SAP solution designed for companies operating in the utilities sector — including electricity distribution, natural gas, and water services.

ALFA Business Technologies has extensive SAP IS-U implementation experience serving major Turkish utility companies, including AYEDAŞ, Bursa Gas, Kayseri Gas, and İPRAGAZ.

## Key IS-U Capabilities

### Device Management
- Smart meter integration and remote reading
- Installation structure management
- Technical master data maintenance

### Billing & Invoicing
- Consumption-based billing cycles
- Rate category management
- Dunning and collections processing

### Customer Service
- Move-in / Move-out processing
- Contract account management
- Customer interaction history

### Financials Integration
- Accounts receivable integration with SAP FI
- Revenue management and reconciliation
- Regulatory reporting

## Industries Served

- **Electricity Distribution** — Grid operators and energy distributors
- **Natural Gas** — Gas distribution and retail companies
- **Water Utilities** — Municipal and private water services
