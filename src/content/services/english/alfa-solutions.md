## Alfa Solutions

ALFA Business Technologies has developed a portfolio of proprietary enterprise solutions addressing Turkish regulatory requirements and operational efficiency needs. All Alfa Solutions are designed for deep SAP integration, automating compliance workflows and eliminating manual data re-entry.

## Our Solution Portfolio

- **ALFA e-Invoice** — GIB-compliant electronic invoice processing with SAP SD/MM/FI integration
- **ALFA e-Ledger** — Automated e-ledger generation and submission for SAP FI
- **ALFA e-Archive** — Digital archiving for legally required documents
- **ALFA e-Reconciliation** — Automated reconciliation of supplier and customer accounts
- **ALFA Organigram** — Organizational chart management integrated with SAP HR
- **SAP Solutions** — SAP PLM, Workflow, Portal, Mobile, and custom software development
