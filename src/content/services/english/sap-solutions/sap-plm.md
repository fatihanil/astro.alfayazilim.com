## SAP PLM — Product Lifecycle Management

SAP PLM (Product Lifecycle Management) enables organizations to manage the complete lifecycle of a product from inception, engineering, and design through manufacture, service, and disposal.

ALFA Business Technologies provides expert SAP PLM implementation services for manufacturing, engineering, and process industry companies.

## SAP PLM Capabilities

### Document Management (DMS)
- Engineering drawing and document versioning
- CAD integration (AutoCAD, SolidWorks, CATIA)
- Classification and metadata management
- Full-text search across all documents

### Engineering Change Management (ECM)
- Change request and change order processing
- Impact analysis across BOM and routing structures
- Multi-level approval workflows
- Revision-controlled document release

### Recipe Management (Process Industries)
- Formula and recipe development
- Specification management
- Regulatory compliance documentation
- Batch traceability

### Quality Management Integration
- Inspection planning linked to PLM objects
- Defect management and 8D problem solving
- Certificate of analysis (CoA) generation

### Project Management
- Engineering project planning in SAP PS
- Resource assignment and milestone tracking
- Integrated cost tracking
