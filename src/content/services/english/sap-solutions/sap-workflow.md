## SAP Workflow — Business Process Automation

SAP Workflow automates multi-step business processes that require coordination between multiple people, systems, or organizational units. ALFA Business Technologies designs and implements custom SAP workflow solutions for a wide range of business scenarios.

## Common Workflow Use Cases

### Finance Workflows
- Purchase order approval (above threshold amounts)
- Invoice exception handling and 3-way match resolution
- Payment run approval
- Cost center budget approval

### HR Workflows
- Leave and absence approval
- Travel request and expense approval
- Employee onboarding task coordination
- Performance review routing

### Procurement Workflows
- Purchase requisition approval
- Vendor onboarding and master data changes
- Contract approval routing

### Document Workflows
- Engineering change request approval
- Quality notification routing
- Document release and publication workflows

### SAP Business Workflow (Classic)
