## SAP Mobile Solutions

Mobile access to SAP data and processes is no longer optional — it's a competitive requirement. ALFA Business Technologies delivers mobile solutions that extend SAP to smartphones, tablets, and field devices.

## Our Mobile Offering

### SAP Fiori Mobile Apps
The SAP Fiori portfolio includes hundreds of mobile-ready apps for common business processes:
- Purchase order approval on the go
- Sales order management
- Warehouse management (WM/EWM)
- Plant maintenance and field service
- Time confirmation for project activities

We configure and deploy standard Fiori apps and customize them for your specific business requirements.

### Custom Native Mobile Apps
For scenarios requiring purpose-built apps beyond standard Fiori:
- Native iOS and Android development
- Cross-platform development (React Native, Flutter)
- Offline-capable apps for field use without connectivity
- Backend integration via SAP OData services or REST APIs

### Offline Mobile Solutions
Field workers often operate without reliable connectivity. Our offline mobile solutions:
- Cache SAP data for field use
- Queue transactions for synchronization when online
- Support barcode/QR code scanning
- Work with rugged industrial devices
