## SAP AI Joule

SAP Joule is SAP's generative AI copilot embedded natively across the SAP product portfolio — including SAP S/4HANA, SAP SuccessFactors, SAP Ariba, and more. It understands business context and helps users work smarter, faster, and with greater accuracy.

## What is SAP Joule?

SAP Joule is not a chatbot — it is a role-aware, context-sensitive AI assistant that:
- Understands your business data and processes
- Responds to natural language requests
- Takes action across SAP applications
- Provides proactive recommendations

## Key Capabilities

### Natural Language Interaction
Users can interact with SAP systems in plain language:
- "Show me the top 10 overdue invoices from last month"
- "Create a purchase order for material XYZ from vendor ABC"
- "What is our current inventory level for product category Electronics?"

### Automated Task Execution
Joule can execute multi-step processes on your behalf:
- Generate reports and summaries
- Initiate approval workflows
- Update master data records
- Draft documents and communications

### Proactive Insights
- Real-time alerts on business anomalies
- Personalized recommendations based on role
- Predictive insights from enterprise data

### Cross-Application Experience
Joule works seamlessly across SAP S/4HANA, SAP BTP, and cloud applications — providing a unified AI experience.
