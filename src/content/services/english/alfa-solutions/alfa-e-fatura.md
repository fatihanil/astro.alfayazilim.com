## ALFA e-Invoice (ALFA e-Fatura)

ALFA has developed a comprehensive e-Invoice solution that helps SAP customers meet the regulations established by Turkey's Revenue Administration (GIB — Gelir İdaresi Başkanlığı). The platform combines robust architecture with superior software development quality to maximize user satisfaction.

## Technical Architecture

- **Java-based signing engine** — Digital signature processing with certificate management
- **Flexible integration** — Works with or without SAP Process Integration (PI)
- **Full SAP integration** — Native integration with SAP SD (Sales), MM (Materials), and FI (Finance) modules
- **Workflow support** — Incoming invoice processing through MM and FI modules via approval workflows

## Key Features

### Outgoing Invoices
- Bulk e-invoice draft creation
- XSD schema validation
- Digital signing and timestamping
- Automated transmission to GIB
- Transmission status tracking
- Outbox management

### Incoming Invoices
- Inbox management and monitoring
- Automatic invoice receipt from GIB
- SAP FI/MM posting integration
- Approval workflow routing

### Compliance
- Full compliance with GIB e-Invoice regulations
- International measurement unit support
- Multi-currency capability
- Audit trail and reporting

## Benefits

- **Eliminate paper invoices** — Fully digital, legally compliant
- **Reduce processing time** — Automated transmission and receipt
- **Minimize errors** — Schema validation before submission
- **SAP-native** — No manual data re-entry required
- **Scalable** — Handles high invoice volumes efficiently
