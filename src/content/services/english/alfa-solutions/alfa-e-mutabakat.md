## ALFA e-Agreement (ALFA e-Mutabakat)

ALFA e-Agreement is a digital reconciliation solution that automates the periodic balance confirmation process between your company and your trading partners (customers and vendors). It eliminates the manual, paper-based reconciliation traditionally done at period-end.

## What is e-Mutabakat?

E-Mutabakat (Electronic Reconciliation) is the digital process of sending and receiving balance confirmation requests to trading partners. Instead of paper letters and manual follow-up, the entire process is automated and tracked digitally.

## How It Works

1. **Data Extraction** — Automatically pulls outstanding balances from SAP FI (AR/AP)
2. **Electronic Sending** — Sends reconciliation requests to partners via email or portal
3. **Online Confirmation** — Partners review and confirm balances through a web portal
4. **Discrepancy Management** — Track and resolve any balance disagreements
5. **Reporting** — Generate reconciliation summary reports for period-end closing

## Key Features

- **SAP FI Integration** — Direct integration with SAP Accounts Receivable (AR) and Accounts Payable (AP)
- **Bulk Processing** — Send reconciliation requests to hundreds of partners simultaneously
- **Partner Portal** — No SAP license required for your trading partners
- **Digital Signatures** — Legally binding electronic confirmation
- **Status Tracking** — Real-time dashboard of pending, confirmed, and disputed items

## Business Benefits

- **Accelerate Period-End Close** — Reduce reconciliation time from weeks to days
- **Reduce Errors** — Eliminate manual data entry and spreadsheet mistakes
- **Audit Ready** — Complete digital trail of all confirmations
- **Cost Savings** — Eliminate postage, printing, and manual processing costs
- **Partner Satisfaction** — Simple, fast online confirmation process for partners
