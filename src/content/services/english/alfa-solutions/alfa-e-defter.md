## ALFA e-Ledger (ALFA e-Defter)

The ALFA e-Ledger solution automates the creation and submission of electronic ledgers (General Ledger and Journal Ledger) to Turkey's Revenue Administration (GIB), in full compliance with Turkish accounting regulations.

## What is e-Defter?

e-Defter (Electronic Ledger) is the digital equivalent of the legally required General Ledger (Büyük Defter) and Journal Ledger (Yevmiye Defteri). All companies subject to e-Invoice obligations are also required to maintain electronic ledgers.

## Key Features

### Automated Ledger Generation
- Automatic extraction of accounting data from SAP FI
- Monthly ledger file creation per GIB specifications
- XBRL format compliance

### Digital Signing & Submission
- Integration with qualified electronic certificate (e-imza) or financial seal (mali mühür)
- Secure transmission to GIB's portal
- Submission confirmation and receipt management

### SAP FI Integration
- Direct read from SAP General Ledger (FI-GL)
- No manual data extraction required
- Reconciliation between SAP and submitted ledger data

### Archiving
- Long-term digital archiving of submitted ledgers
- Easy retrieval for tax audits
- Integration with ALFA e-Archive solution

## Compliance & Security

- Full compliance with GIB e-Defter regulations
- Encrypted data transmission
- Complete audit trail for every submission
- Retention management per Turkish legal requirements
