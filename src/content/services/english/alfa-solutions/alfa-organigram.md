## ALFA Organigram

ALFA Organigram is a proprietary solution that provides dynamic, interactive organizational chart visualization directly from your SAP HR (HCM) data. It bridges the gap between your SAP organizational structure and a user-friendly visual representation accessible to business users.

## Key Features

### Dynamic Org Chart Visualization
- Real-time org chart generated from SAP HR organizational structure
- Drill-down navigation from company to department to individual position
- Search and filter by name, department, or position

### SAP HCM Integration
- Direct data pull from SAP Organizational Management (OM)
- Personnel Administration (PA) data synchronization
- Automatic updates when organizational changes occur in SAP

### Role & Position Management
- Clear display of reporting lines and hierarchies
- Position vacancy tracking
- Headcount visualization by department

### Export & Sharing
- Export org charts to PDF, PowerPoint, or image formats
- Share read-only links with stakeholders outside SAP
- Embed org chart views in intranet portals

### Access Control
- Role-based access to org chart data
- Sensitive data masking for non-HR users
- Audit trail for org chart changes

## Use Cases

- **Onboarding** — Help new employees understand the organization
- **Management Reporting** — Visual headcount and structure reports
- **HR Planning** — Workforce planning and succession visualization
- **Mergers & Acquisitions** — Visualize combined organizational structures
