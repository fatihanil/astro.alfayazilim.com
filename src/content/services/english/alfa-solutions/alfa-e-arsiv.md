## ALFA e-Archive (ALFA e-Arşiv)

ALFA e-Archive is a comprehensive digital archiving solution that extends SAP's document management capabilities to meet Turkish legal requirements for electronic document retention, including e-Archive Invoice (e-Arşiv Fatura) obligations.

## e-Archive Invoice (e-Arşiv Fatura)

For businesses that issue invoices to non-e-Invoice users (B2C or non-registered companies), e-Archive Invoice is the mandatory GIB-compliant solution. ALFA e-Archive handles:

- Automatic generation of e-Archive invoices from SAP SD
- Digital signing and timestamping
- Transmission to GIB (for revenue over threshold)
- Customer delivery via email or printed copy
- Long-term archiving in GIB-compliant format

## SAP Document Archiving

Beyond e-invoices, ALFA e-Archive provides archiving for all SAP business documents:

- Purchase orders and contracts
- Delivery notes and goods receipts
- Financial postings and accounting documents
- HR records and personnel documents

## Key Features

- **SAP Integration** — Direct archiving from SAP without manual export
- **Legal Compliance** — Meets Turkish e-Archive regulations
- **Fast Retrieval** — Instant search and document access
- **Secure Storage** — Encrypted, tamper-evident archiving
- **Long-Term Retention** — 10+ year document retention support
