## Careers at ALFA

ALFA Business Technologies believes that success stems from satisfied employees who are open to development and growth. We operate on a simple yet powerful principle: **Happy Employee, Happy Employer**.

## Why Work at ALFA?

- Work alongside experienced SAP consultants and managers
- Engage in diverse, challenging enterprise projects
- Continuous training and professional development support
- Dynamic, evolving work environment
- Opportunities to grow within Turkey's leading SAP partner

## Career Development

At ALFA, employees expand their expertise by collaborating with experienced managers across diverse projects. The company provides training, development support, and encouragement to help every team member maximize their professional goals.

## Our Internship Program

New trainees participate in a **three-month intensive training program** under expert consultants, focusing on:

- SAP-based software development
- SAP implementation methodologies
- Enterprise IT best practices
- Hands-on project exposure

## Hiring Requirements

- Minimum bachelor's degree required
- Non-discriminatory hiring policy (no restrictions on age or gender)
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
- Interest in enterprise IT and SAP ecosystems
